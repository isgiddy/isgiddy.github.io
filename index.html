Senior Researcher · SOCCO, CSIR · South Africa

Isabelle Giddy

Ocean turbulence · observational oceanography · earth system science

I'm curious about how winds and waves stir the upper ocean. I chase turbulence at the air–sea boundary with autonomous gliders and surface robots, then use models and theory to understand what that mixing means for heat, carbon and our climate.

Interesting things happen at boundaries.

More about me CV Turbulence Jupyter Book

Research interests

  • Air–sea interactionHow momentum, heat and carbon cross the ocean surface.
  • Boundary layer turbulenceMixing in the ocean surface boundary layer, observed and modelled.
  • Wind–wave–current interactionsStokes drift, Langmuir turbulence and wave-driven mixing.
  • Autonomous ocean observingGliders and surface robots that measure turbulence where ships rarely go.
